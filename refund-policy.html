Refund Policy

Our commitment to fair and transparent refunds for all Veducore plans and services.

Last updated: October 2026 Applicable in India

Please read this policy before making any payment to Veducore. By completing a purchase, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Overview

At Veducore, we strive to deliver a high-quality platform experience. We understand that circumstances can change, and we have designed this refund policy to be fair to both parties. This policy applies to all subscription plans — Basic, Standard and Premium.

2. Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a refund under the following conditions:

2.1 Eligible for Refund

  • You request a refund within 7 days of your initial payment and have not actively used the platform (no student enrolments, no data uploaded)
  • There is a technical failure on our end that prevents you from accessing the platform and we are unable to resolve it within 7 business days
  • You were charged incorrectly due to a billing error on our part
  • The plan you purchased was materially different from what was described at the time of purchase

2.2 Not Eligible for Refund

  • Requests made after 7 days of the initial payment date
  • Accounts that have been actively used (students enrolled, classes conducted, data added)
  • Partial refunds for unused days within a billing period
  • Refunds due to change of mind after onboarding has begun
  • Add-on services, custom development or one-time setup fees
  • Payments made for renewal of existing subscriptions

Note: Annual subscription fees are non-refundable after the 7-day window, regardless of usage. We recommend starting with a demo before committing to an annual plan.

3. How to Request a Refund

Follow these steps to submit a refund request:

  • 1
    Email us Send a refund request to veducore@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request – [Your Institute Name]"
  • 2
    Include details Provide your registered email, payment transaction ID, date of payment and reason for the refund request
  • 3
    Wait for review Our team will acknowledge your request within 2 business days and review eligibility
  • 4
    Receive decision You will receive a final decision within 5–7 business days of submitting your request
  • 5
    Refund processed Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7–10 business days

4. Refund Method

All approved refunds will be credited back to the original payment method used at the time of purchase:

  • Credit/Debit card payments — refunded to the same card
  • UPI / Net banking — refunded to the originating account
  • Bank transfer — refunded to the source bank account

We do not issue refunds via cash, cheque or alternate payment methods.

5. Cancellation Policy

You may cancel your Veducore subscription at any time from your account settings or by contacting our support team. Upon cancellation:

  • Your access continues until the end of the current billing period
  • No further charges will be made after cancellation
  • Data is retained for 30 days post-cancellation, after which it may be permanently deleted
  • Cancellation does not automatically trigger a refund — please refer to the eligibility criteria above

6. Disputes

If you disagree with our refund decision, you may escalate by replying to your original refund request email. We will re-review within 5 business days. We are committed to resolving all disputes fairly and promptly.
